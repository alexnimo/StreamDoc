# StreamDoc design brief (taste-skill inspired)

**Site of truth for look and feel:**
- Strong typography and minimal UI.
- Dark mode default.
- High-contrast but not harsh.
- No gradients or shiny surfaces.
- Focus: cards, CTAs, whitespace, and sharp borders.
